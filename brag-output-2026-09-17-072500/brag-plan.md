# Brag Plan: Demi's Portfolio

## What is this app?
A full-stack, monorepo portfolio with a Next.js frontend, Express backend, and React CMS, all wrapped in an aggressively pink, sparkly, "girly" CSS aesthetic.

## The angle
The contrast between serious, heavy-duty software engineering (monorepo, PostgreSQL, Next.js, Express) and the unapologetically sparkly, floating-heart, pink-gradient visual identity. It's over-engineered and over-sparkled.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A deadpan, serious text reveal: "Full-Stack Monorepo Architecture." Then, the background instantly explodes into pink gradients, floating hearts 💖, and sparkles ✨.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A serious tech stack list (Next.js, Express, PostgreSQL, React CMS) appearing one by one, but each is styled as a cute pink `.tech-tag`.
- A project card sliding in with a pink gradient top bar, hovering with a soft pink shadow.
- The contrast of "Database: PostgreSQL" written in a delicate, gradient font.

## Outro / punchline
"Demi Taylor Nimmo. Serious engineering. ✨💖"

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only (portfolio site). We will rely on the strongest visual elements: the hero gradient, floating hearts, and project cards.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic
- Creative direction: Serious full-stack engineering meets aggressively girly aesthetic
- Interpretation: Fast cuts between serious technical terms and explosive pink visuals. High energy, leaning into the absurdity of the contrast.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: linear-gradient(135deg, #f8f4ff 0%, #fff8f0 50%, #ffb3ba 100%)
- Accent: linear-gradient(45deg, #ff6b9d, #c44569)
- Text: #2d3436 (dark) and #ff6b9d (primary pink)
- Display font: Playfair Display
- Body font: Poppins
- Strongest visual element: The floating hearts (💖) and sparkles (✨) over the soft lavender/peach gradient.

## Share copy (draft)
A full-stack Next.js/Express monorepo, but make it aggressively sparkly. ✨💖

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer
- Music: upbeat, high-energy hyper-pop or bouncy electronic track
- Music treatment: hard start, high volume, fast fade out at the end
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time. Target strong cues for the tech stack reveals and the initial sparkle explosion.
- Audio-reactive treatment: expressive; hearts and sparkles pulse slightly to the beat
- SFX posture: dense; sparkly magic chimes for reveals, heavy bass/whoosh for the serious text
- Audio-coupled moments: the tech stack tags popping in one-by-one on the beat
- Restraint rule: do not use corporate/bland swooshes. Needs to sound magical or heavy.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Setup — 3s
Black screen. White, monospace, serious text types out: "ARCHITECTURE: FULL-STACK MONOREPO".
Sequential/interaction: yes — text types out character by character.
Audio intent: serious, heavy, mechanical typing.
Audio-coupled idea: type the hook with heavy mechanical keyboard ticks.
Music: none yet (silence for the setup).
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Reveal — 4s
The screen explodes into the `hero-girly` gradient (lavender/cream/peach). Floating hearts 💖 and sparkles ✨ fill the screen. The text changes to a beautiful serif font (Playfair Display) in a pink gradient: "But make it cute."
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: sudden burst of high-energy hyper-pop music and magical chimes.
Audio-coupled idea: beat-aligned reveal of the gradient and text.
Music: upbeat hyper-pop starts hard.
Transition mood: fast wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Tech Stack — 5s
A sleek project card (`.card-girly`) appears. Inside, serious tech terms appear one by one, but they are wrapped in cute pink `.tech-tag` pills: "Next.js", "Express.js", "PostgreSQL", "React CMS".
Sequential/interaction: yes — the 4 tech tags pop in one by one.
Audio intent: bouncy, rhythmic pops matching the beat.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card sequence on the beat.
Music: upbeat hyper-pop continues.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 6s
The screen clears to the soft gradient. Large serif text: "Demi Taylor Nimmo". Below it, smaller text: "Software Engineer ✨".
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: triumphant, sparkly finish.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: upbeat hyper-pop hits a final chord and fades out.
Transition mood: fade to black.

**Music mood for this video:** chaotic / hyper-pop
**Audio summary:** Starts with serious, heavy silence, then explodes into high-energy, sparkly hyper-pop with rhythmic pops for the tech reveals.
